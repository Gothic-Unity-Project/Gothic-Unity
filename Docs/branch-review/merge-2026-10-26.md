## Rules used

Same as [merge-2026-10-22](merge-2026-10-22.md): main's structure wins, our behavior is ported into it. HVR stays the
default backend (`VrInteractionBackend: 0`), so nothing changes for the player on HVR.

## Moved onto main's ports

- Items, NPCs, NPC loot, focus, dig spots, lock picking: `VrGrabArgs` / `VrInteractable` instead of HVR events.
- Backpack: NPC loot and trader copies, [E] labels, take all, trade returns on main's slot refresh; copies use
  `VRBackpack.PrefabPath` (`BackPack XRI` on the Xri backend).
- Chests: the contents tracking for saves (put in / taken out) moved from `VRVobContainerPhysicsChest` to
  `VrChestSlots`, so both backends keep it.
- Swimming: main's version plus our G2 water, BS_SWIM/BS_DIVE and null-safe sounds.
- Fists, holster removal by scripts, knockout drops: `VRWeaponService` on `IVrRig` / `IVrInput` / `IVrHaptics`.
- `VRPlayerController` is gone: footsteps, BS_WALK/RUN/SNEAK, the speed potion and the slower sneak sprint are in the
  new `VRHeroMovement` (any backend); comfort and locking in `VRContextInteractionService` (`LockPlayerInPlace` keeps
  `allowTurning`).
- Immersion menu: main's "Physical hands" option in row 1, our options below.

## New port members

`IVrRig.GetHandVisual`, `IsCrouching`, `CameraYOffset`; `IVrHaptics.Vibrate(side, amplitude, duration)`;
`IVrUiPointers.GetPointerLines`; `IVrInteractableBackend.SetupColliders(isChildLinked)`. Components created at runtime
read buttons, velocities and vibrations through the static `VrControls`.

## Still HVR only

Behind `GOTHIC_HVR_INSTALLED` until ported: favorites ring, rune casting, telekinesis and spell targeting (force
grabber bags), stack splitting, trade counter, pickpocket, hero body, transformation, bow and crossbow, grab bars.
`VRContextInteractionService` references some of them, so `Gothic.VR` doesn't compile without HVR yet.
