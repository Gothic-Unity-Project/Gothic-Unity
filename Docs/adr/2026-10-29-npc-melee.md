# ADR: NPCs fight in melee, knock out or kill like in Gothic

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-29

## Context

Human NPCs didn't fight at all: their attacks were skipped since a stopgap from June. Monsters swung at their targets, but their swings never hurt anybody. A beaten NPC always died, nobody got experience, and the scripts' part of a death (loot, quest variables) never ran. BoroBongo's experimental branch has all of this working and play-tested, so this ports its melee part (slices 8 and 9 of the branch split plan) onto main.

## Decisions

1. **NPCs and monsters attack with Gothic's fight AI tables, and a swing hits at its animation's hit frame.** An attack asked for during a swing's combo window continues the same animation with its next part, like the engine. Not chosen: hits by the weapon's bone touching the target, which Gothic does; for now a swing hits within the weapon's reach and in front of the attacker.
2. **Damage is the engine's formula.** Strength plus the weapon's damage minus the target's protection per damage type, with Gothic 1's critical hits and Gothic 2's hit chance. The hero's damage comes from the weapon in his VR hand, as it isn't equipped in VR.
3. **A human beaten in melee is knocked out unless it is hostile to its attacker,** the engine's rule. It falls down, runs the scripts' unconscious state and gets up again; a hit on it while it lies there kills it. Monsters and orcs always die.
4. **Every death runs the scripts' death state with the killer,** so the hero gets experience for his own kills only, and quest variables and loot follow script deaths too.
5. **The knocked out hero lies down for 10 seconds,** his attackers calm down and put their weapons away. He can't die yet: there is no death screen in VR.
6. **A state started with "end the current one" runs the old state's end first,** like the engine. Before, a Gothic 1 dialog that ends in a fight skipped the dialog's end and the hero stayed invincible.
7. **An NPC's attitude to the hero set by the scripts counts.** Before, it came back as "none" whenever only the permanent attitude was set, so hostile bandits didn't attack.
8. **A knocked out or killed NPC puts its weapon away at once.** In the original, the weapon falls to the ground, and an NPC that gets up picks it up again. Not chosen for now: our NPCs can't pick up items yet, so guards would stay unarmed for good. The weapon stays in the inventory and can be looted from a body.

## Consequences

- Guards, bandits, orcs and monsters fight the hero and each other, brawls among friends end in a knockout, kills give experience.
- Still open: hits of archers and mages (they fight but don't hurt), parrying and blocking, finishing moves, the hero's death, fleeing, NPCs reacting to the hero's drawn weapon, the hero's running or sneaking for the scripts, and weapons falling to the ground on a knockout or death.
- Each hit asks the NPCs around whether they saw it, a short loop over all NPCs in the world per hit.
- A kill costs about 8 milliseconds in the frame of the hit and 6.5 milliseconds for the death state in the frame after, measured in the editor in the Old Camp with about 45 NPCs around. Not measured on a headset yet; on a Quest 2 a kill likely costs a dropped frame.
