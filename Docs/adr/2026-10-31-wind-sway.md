# ADR: Trees and grass sway in the wind

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-31

## Context

In Gothic 2 trees, bushes and grass sway in the wind; Gothic 1's worlds have no such objects. The objects carry the engine's wind mode (tree or grass) and mostly no strength of their own. Every swaying object costs a little extra work per vertex, which matters on the Quest 2.

## Decisions

1. **Objects with the wind mode lean along the wind by their height and swing back and forth,** like the reference engine: trees by 3 per cent of their height, grass by its strength. Objects of either mode without a strength of their own swing like a tree, so Gothic 2's forests move; objects whose swing would be invisible stay as they are.
2. **The swing takes Gothic.ini's wind cycle,** and objects farther along the wind swing a little later.
3. **The swaying is an option, the game's own "wind enabled" setting, in Gothic's graphics menu.** Off, the objects use their normal materials and cost nothing. Not chosen: the immersion menu, which is full once the open branches are in.
4. **On the Quest it starts off,** on the PC on; an existing Gothic.ini value wins (Gothic 2's ini ships with wind on).

## Consequences

- Turning the option on or off takes effect at once, without reloading the world.
