# ADR: Save and load - Unity state next to ZenKit's save

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-31
- Technical details: SaveGameService, UnityCustomSave, WorldItemSaveDomain, NpcInitializerDomain (Core); VRHolsterSaveState, VrChestSlots (VR); DeveloperConfig "Save and Load"

## Context

ZenKit writes Gothic's save (SAVEDAT, WORLD.SAV): the Daedalus variables and the world's VOBs. Our runtime keeps more state than that, and some of it ZenKit can't hold:
- NPCs placed by our spawning;
- items moved by hand;
- the hero's equipped items as VR objects;
- holsters;
- worlds the player left.

Loading only WORLD.SAV brought back NPCs at their spawn points, dead NPCs standing, items duplicated or gone, and an empty hero.

## Decisions

1. **Two layers.** ZenKit's save stays the Gothic part. A `UNITYSAVE.json` next to it holds what our runtime needs:
   - the hero (position, equipped items, talents, holsters);
   - NPC states per world;
   - NPCs spawned mid-game;
   - items and chests that differ from the world file.

   Not chosen: writing everything into WORLD.SAV, because ZenKit has no place for it and other tools must still read the save.
2. **Every visited world keeps its state.** When the player leaves a world, its NPCs and items are stored. Coming back, in the same session or after a load, finds them as left.
3. **NPCs restore from a new-game baseline plus a delta** (`UNITYNPCINIT.json` + the saved changes). The world's INIT_ function runs again with NPC insertion and triggers suppressed, so script aliases (`Hlp_GetNpc`) point to the restored NPCs. This stays behind `EnableSaveLoadSystem` (off by default, as on the source branch) until it's tested more widely.
4. **Dead stays dead.** An NPC dead at save time lies dead on load, and its ZS_Dead runs once, so quest variables are set in the fresh VM.
5. **Items in the hands go into the backpack before saving.** Otherwise they are neither in the inventory nor in the world.

## Consequences

- Movers and triggers (doors, lifts) aren't saved yet. Their state comes with the movers/triggers PR.
- Older saves without UNITYSAVE.json load with the Gothic part only.
