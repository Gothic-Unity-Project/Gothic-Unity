# ADR: Magic - NPCs and the VR hero cast spells

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-30

## Context

Nobody could cast a spell. Mages drew nothing and fought with their fists or not at all, runes and scrolls were dead weight in the hero's backpack, and the scripts asked the engine about spells and always got "none". In Gothic the scripts own most of magic: they decide how much mana a spell needs, when it goes off and what it does to its victim; the engine runs the casting, flies the projectiles and takes the damage off.

## Decisions

1. **The scripts decide, like in the engine.** A cast invests mana in portions at the spell's own pace, and for each portion the scripts answer whether to go on, raise the spell's level, cast or stop. In Gothic 1 the engine takes one mana per portion, in Gothic 2 the spell's cast function takes it. Spells that do their work in the scripts (heal, sleep, teleport, transformations) therefore work without extra code.
2. **Projectiles fly, everything else reaches its target through the scripts.** Firebolts and fireballs fly at the engine's speed of 10 m/s at their target and hit the first NPC they pass, never their caster; walls stop them. Their damage is the spell's damage per level times the level reached, against the victim's protection; immune damage types do nothing. Gothic 1 lets the spell's target type decide whom it touches, Gothic 2 asks its scripts, which can also double, halve or forbid killing (shock spells knock out). Spells that don't fly tell their target which spell hit it, and its scripts react (fear, freeze, sleep).
3. **NPC mages use the game's magic fight moves.** Within 35 m they cast, otherwise they close in. Every cast plays the spell's invest and cast animations and keeps the caster facing its enemy. A used-up scroll goes, the last one puts the magic away.
4. **The VR hero casts with the trigger.** Holding a rune or scroll and pressing its trigger is Gothic's action key: mana goes in while it is held, letting go casts, unless the scripts cast or stop earlier. The spell flies at the NPC closest to where the hand points (within 15 degrees and 35 m), or straight ahead. A rune above the hero's magic circle refuses with the scripts' message and an error buzz; every portion of mana ticks the hand, the cast buzzes. A used scroll vanishes from the hand.
5. **The look comes from the game's own effects.** The particles of the spell's visual effect fly with it and appear where it hits. Lights, sounds, meshes and invest stages of the effects are not shown yet.

## Consequences

- Mages fight with magic, and the hero can use runes and scrolls in VR.
- Area spells (fire rain, storms) only show their effect and hurt nobody yet; summoning and transformation spells do what their scripts do, which in Gothic needs more engine support (new NPCs, the transformation itself).
- The hero has no aiming aid beyond the target choice by hand direction; a crosshair or focus marker may come later.
