# ADR: VR melee - block by weapon contact, fists, stab finish

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-02
- Technical details: VRHeroBlock, VRWeaponService (fists), VRFistHitbox, VrStabFinishDomain, VRWeaponAttackAdapter (VR); FightService hero block, AbstractMeshBuilder fitted melee colliders, ItemColliderFittingDomain (Core); Gothic.ini [GOTHIC_VR_IMMERSION] heroBlock, stabFinish (no DeveloperConfig flags)

## Context

In VR the hero fights with his real hands. Gothic's own rules are made for a keyboard: a parry is a button, a fist is a mode, and a finishing move is an animation. On main, the hero could only swing a sword. A block held anywhere in front of him would have stopped every hit, and weapons got one box collider along the mesh axes.

## Decisions

1. **A block counts only where the weapons touch.** Both hands on a melee weapon and both triggers held make a parry, as in Gothic. While blocking, `VRHeroBlock` samples each nearby NPC's drawn weapon along its length (for fists, the hand), and `FightService` blocks a hit only if the weapons touched within the last 0.5 s. A hit that reaches the body first waits up to 0.5 s for the touch. Not chosen: an angle cone in front of the hero, which blocked hits from above the NPC's head and hits the hero never parried.
2. **Fists are a gesture, not a mode switch.** An empty hand with grip + trigger held is a fist. It punches with hand speed and does the engine's fist damage (strength, blunt).
3. **A finishing move is a stab kept in the body.** The blade thrust into an unconscious NPC (speed along the blade) and kept there for 2 s kills, through the same `FightFinishingMove` as an NPC's `AI_FinishingMove`. A slash doesn't finish anyone. Not chosen: a twist of the wrist, which testing showed was too finicky.
4. **Weapon hitboxes follow the weapon's parts.** Melee weapons get oriented boxes per part (blade, guard, grip, axe head) from `ItemColliderFittingDomain`, computed once per mesh. The hitbox is what the swing hits, so it must not be full of air.
5. **A knocked-out hero drops what he holds**, like Gothic's knockout.

## Consequences

- The swing threshold (2 m/s on average over 0.5 s) isn't verified against a trembling hand yet. A `[VrSwing]` log shows the speeds of every swing so it can be tuned.
- Damage numbers over the head come with the UI PR, and NPC body bounds come with the movement PR.
- Nothing new is saved. A block or a stab in progress is lost on save and load.
