# ADR: VR magic - runes held in both hands, thrown spells, transformations

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Technical details: VRRuneCaster, VRChainLightning, VRSpellEffect, VRTransformPuppet, VRTransformService, VRPlayerService (spell targeting, telekinesis), VRWeaponService.SetRuneReadied (VR); VmCacheService.GetSpellDamagePerLevel, GlobalEventDispatcher.HeroScriptTeleporting, IVisualFxLight (Core); DeveloperConfig EnableThrowableSpells, EnableTwoHandSpellCharge; VRConstants.Magic (MinSpellCastSeconds, SpellThrowSpeedMultiplier, SpellThrowFullSwingSpeed)

## Context

In Gothic the hero readies a rune and holds the cast key: mana goes in tick by tick (`Spell_ProcessMana`), and letting go early asks the script (`Spell_ProcessMana_Release`). In VR there is no cast key and no camera aim. The hero holds the rune in his hands. NPC magic, spell effects, burning and hero spell states (sleep, freeze) already came with PR #479.

## Decisions

1. **A rune or scroll held with both hands casts.** The holding hand's trigger works like the cast key: pressed it aims and charges, held it charges, let go early the script decides. The mana ticks follow the spell's own `time_per_mana`. The Daedalus protocol is the engine's: `Spell_ProcessMana`, `Spell_ProcessMana_Release` and `Spell_Cast_<name>`. G2 scripts pay the mana themselves, and the VOB follows the instance after every call.
2. **Aimed spells aim with the hand.** The rune hand's distance grab reaches the spell's range and picks the NPC. While a spell is active, grabbing an NPC doesn't start a dialog.
3. **Projectile spells are thrown** (`EnableThrowableSpells`): charge in one hand, swing and let go. The swing gives direction and speed. A weak throw arcs down, and only a real throw homes in. Not chosen: flying where the camera looks, which needed no hand movement at all.
4. **Charged spells grow only with both hands together** (`EnableTwoHandSpellCharge`, 30 cm): a kamehameha. It's a VR decision, so it has a switch.
5. **Damage per level, like the engine** (`damage_per_level * level`, at most 4). One rule for the hero and the NPCs (`VmCacheService.GetSpellDamagePerLevel`), and only SPELL_BAD spells hurt.
6. **Instant spells glow for `VRConstants.Magic.MinSpellCastSeconds` (2 s)** before they go off, so their effects can be seen. Throws aren't delayed.
7. **Scrolls are used up like in Gothic.** That covers a cast or a throw. The "Single-use scrolls" Immersion option (on by default) can switch it off.
8. **A transformation scroll makes the hero the monster**. The monster follows the player without AI, the camera goes down to its eyes, and the hero's guild is the monster's. The orb hand's trigger transforms back, and so does leaving the world (a level change or loading a save).
9. **A teleport spell puts the held items into the backpack** (`HeroScriptTeleporting`, raised by `AI_Teleport`). Scene teleports and loads don't.
10. **The hero falls asleep under a sleep spell**, with Gothic's own animations.

## Consequences

- `VRRuneCaster` holds the Daedalus spell protocol in a VR adapter (about 1,000 lines). Effects, throwing and telekinesis are its workers (`VRSpellFx`, `VRSpellThrow`, `VRTelekinesisSpell`, map in `Docs/vr-magic.md`). A Core `HeroSpellCastDomain` for the protocol is a follow-up after the favorites ring PR. Flat would need it.
- Telekinesis skips Daedalus: no mana and no scroll use. This is a known gap.
- A transformation isn't saved. Leaving the world transforms back first.
- The transformation's picture-in-picture camera renders every frame. A Quest measurement and a lower rate are a follow-up.
- The favorites ring comes with the next PR (the `RingHand` hook stays).
