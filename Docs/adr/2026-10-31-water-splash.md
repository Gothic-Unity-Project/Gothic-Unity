# ADR: Splashes

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-31

## Context

In Gothic whoever falls or jumps into deep water makes a splash, with the splash particles and sound. Here the water stayed still.

## Decisions

1. **A splash comes when the hero or an NPC gets into water deep enough to swim straight away,** from a fall or a jump. Walking in passes knee-deep water first and doesn't splash.
2. **The hero's splash sits where his view meets the surface,** an NPC's at its position.
