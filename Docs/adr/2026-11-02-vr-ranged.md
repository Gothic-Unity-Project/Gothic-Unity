# ADR: VR ranged - drawn bows, crossbows reloaded by hand, script-rejected enemies forgotten

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-11-02
- Technical details: VRBow, VRCrossbow, VRCrossbowString, VRRangedService, VRVobItem, VRWeaponService.GetHeldRangedState (VR); MorphMeshCacheService.TryGetMorphDataForVertices, VobItemColliderCacheCreatorDomain + VobItemFittedColliderCacheCreatorDomain (pre-cached bow/crossbow colliders), ItemColliderSlicingDomain, VobItemColliderBuilder, NpcAiService.ExecuteAssessEnemy (Core); Immersion option `crossbowReload` (VRConstants)

## Context

In Gothic the hero aims a bow with the camera and shoots with a button. In VR he holds the bow in his hands. NPC archers already shoot real arrows through `ProjectileService` (PR #479). The hero needs the same projectiles, ammo and damage, but driven by his hands.

## Decisions

1. **One projectile path for the hero and NPCs.** `VRBow` and `VRCrossbow` shoot through `VRRangedService`, a thin facade over `ProjectileService`: the same munition rule (1 item per shot), flight, damage and missed arrows lying in the world.
2. **A bow is drawn by its string.** The free hand grips the string near the nock and pulls. Speed and damage grow with the draw. The limbs bend with Gothic's own `S_SHOOT` morph animation. Its frames come from the vertex mapping made when the mesh is built from the morph mesh (the same frames as for heads); only if that mapping doesn't fit the mesh are the vertices matched by position.
3. **A crossbow is reloaded by hand.** After a shot the string sits at the prod (Gothic's `S_RELAX` shape). The free hand holds it with the trigger and pulls it back along the stock until it locks. The "Crossbow" Immersion option switches back to an automatic reload. A crossbow needs both hands, except for a trained crossbowman (60 %+, G1: also the master talent).
4. **Bows and crossbows get fitted colliders,** like melee weapons. Morph mesh items had no collider at all (they fell through the ground). Which item is a bow or crossbow comes from its flags (ITEM_KAT_FF, ITEM_CROSSBOW), not its mesh name. They are fitted while pre-caching, like melee weapons: most of them have no MRM, so the pre-cache fits the mesh of their morph mesh, the one the item is built from. If the fit finds no part, boxes are sliced along the mesh. Only an item without pre-cached colliders (another morph mesh item, an old cache) gets them from its mesh when it spawns, once per mesh. The static cache version went to 7, so existing caches are rebuilt.
5. **An enemy the script rejects is forgotten.** `ExecutePerception` returns the perception function's result (null when it didn't run). When G2's `B_AssessEnemy` returns FALSE (enemy override, a party member's 15 m, height), the NPC forgets that enemy and looks again next tick. Before, `UpdateEnemyNpc` kept the rejected enemy while it stayed valid, and it blocked every other one. G2 Cavalorn fought the cave bandits only when hit, so always with the sword (`B_SelectWeapon` takes the bow only beyond 10 m with a reason to kill). Now he rejects a bandit at 19 m, accepts one at 12 m and shoots (play-tested). Not while the NPC fights with a drawn weapon.
   This is our rule, not OpenGothic's: its `perceptionProcess` ignores the script's answer, and its `updateNearestEnemy` lets a closer enemy replace the kept one. Not chosen for now: the closer-enemy rescan in `UpdateEnemyNpc`, because its stickiness fixed scavengers that switched targets (follow-up).

## Consequences

- G1's `B_AssessEnemy` has no return value: nothing changes there.
- Perception functions with a return value no longer leave it on the VM stack.
- Cavalorn is play-tested: he rejects a bandit at 19 m, accepts one at 12 m and shoots. The `[Perc] AssessEnemy` log shows each accept or reject with distance, height, attitude and the AIV flags.
- Nothing new is saved. A drawn bow or a half-pulled crossbow string is lost on save and load. A crossbow's cocked state lives on the item and is reset to cocked when it comes out of a holster or the backpack.
