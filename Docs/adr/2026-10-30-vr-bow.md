# ADR: The VR hero shoots bows and crossbows

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-30

## Context

The hero could carry bows and crossbows but not shoot them; only swords and fists worked in VR. In Gothic the hero draws, aims with the help of the focus and shoots an arrow from his inventory.

## Decisions

1. **The trigger shoots.** A bow draws while the trigger of the hand holding it is held and shoots when it is let go; it is fully drawn after a moment, which a buzz tells. A crossbow shoots on the press and needs a moment to reload. A real two-handed draw (grabbing the string) can come later; the trigger works with one hand and in the simulator.
2. **Arrows come from the inventory and fly like the engine's.** Each shot takes one arrow or bolt of the weapon's munition; without any it doesn't shoot and the hand gets the error buzz. The arrow flies at the engine's speed on a falling arc from the hand where it points, slower from a bow not fully drawn, and hits the first NPC it passes.
3. **The player's aim replaces the hit chance.** NPC archers still hit by their chance at the distance, the hero hits what his arrow touches. Damage follows the engine's ranged formula (weapon damage against protection, Gothic 2 adds dexterity, Gothic 1 has critical hits by talent). Arrows kill rather than knock out, for everybody.

## Consequences

- The hero can fight at range, and hunting with a bow works.
- Missed arrows stay in the world for a while but can't be picked up again yet.
- There is no aiming aid; a trajectory hint may come with the two-handed draw.
