# ADR: Rooms and intruders

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-30

## Context

In Gothic, huts and houses belong to guilds or people. Walking into one, its owners notice and warn the intruder or throw him out. The scripts give rooms to guilds when a world starts, but here that did nothing: no room was known, nobody noticed the hero walking in.

## Decisions

1. **A room is the part of the world the floor under the feet belongs to, like in the engine.** The world's polygons say which room they belong to. Their floors are put into a grid once per world, which takes a few milliseconds; outdoors there is no room.
2. **The scripts' room functions work:** giving a room to a guild, the guild of the room the hero is in and was in before, the guild of an NPC's room, and whether an NPC is in the hero's room. Outdoors counts as no room.
3. **When the hero walks into another room or out of one, the NPCs around notice it,** like in the engine, unless he sneaks. Their scripts decide what happens: a warning, being thrown out, a fight.
4. **The hero's room is checked four times a second.**

## Consequences

- Break-ins are noticed again: owners react to the hero in their huts.
- Rooms aren't saved: the scripts assign them anew whenever a world starts, as in the original.
- Doorways count to the room only where the floor polygons reach, so standing in a door is usually outside.
