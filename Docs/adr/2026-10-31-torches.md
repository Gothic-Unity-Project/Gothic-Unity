# ADR: Torches

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-31

## Context

In Gothic the hero lights a torch from his inventory and carries it through the mines, crypts and the night. In VR a torch was a stick: it could be held but never lit.

## Decisions

1. **The trigger of the hand holding a torch lights it or puts it out,** like using a torch in Gothic. The flame, its light range and its sound come from the game's own burning torch item, at the end of the stick farther from the grip.
2. **A lit torch lights the world around it and keeps burning when dropped,** like a burning torch thrown on the ground.
3. **Water puts it out for good:** it becomes a burnt torch, as when the hero dives with a torch in Gothic. A burnt torch can't be lit again.
4. **Put away, it is an ordinary torch again,** since the inventory holds no burning torches.

## Consequences

- A loaded game starts with every torch out.
- A burnt torch keeps the look of the torch it was until it is picked up.
