# ADR: Trading

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-30

## Context

Traders had dialog entries like "Show me your goods", but choosing one did nothing: there was no trading at all. In Gothic such an entry opens the trade screen after the trader's lines: his goods on one side, the hero's on the other. Gothic 2 trades with gold, Gothic 1 with ore.

## Decisions

1. **Trading follows the engine.** Each item changes hands the moment it is bought or sold. The hero pays the item's value and gets its value times the game's trade multiplier when he sells (Gothic 2 pays less than the value, Gothic 1 the full value). Equipped items and the money itself aren't traded. The trader's own money doesn't change, as in the engine.
2. **In VR the trade screen is a panel in front of the player, used with the UI laser like the menus.** The trader's goods on the left, the hero's on the right, each with its price, the hero's money below. A click buys or sells one piece; arrows page through long lists; X ends trading. Items the player can't afford are greyed out. Not chosen: a counter with real items to hand over, which is nicer in VR but much more work; it can replace the panel later on top of the same trading rules.
3. **The dialog waits while trading and goes on afterwards with its choices.** Ending the dialog ends trading too.
4. **Items the hero holds in his hands aren't offered for sale**, so a sold item can't stay in the hand.

## Consequences

- Traders, the money and the trade multiplier work as in the original, in both games.
- Buying or selling a stack takes one click per piece.
- Flat mode can use the same trading rules with its own screen later.
