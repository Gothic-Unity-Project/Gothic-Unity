# ADR: NPCs flee

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-30

## Context

Cowards, beaten NPCs and weak animals run away in Gothic: their scripts start the flee state, which tells them again and again to flee from their enemy. That instruction did nothing here, so fleeing NPCs stood still in front of the hero.

## Decisions

1. **Fleeing works like in the engine: turn away from the enemy and run, a short step at a time.** The script's flee state repeats it until the NPC is far enough.
2. **A wall right ahead turns the escape sideways,** to the free direction closest to straight away. Not chosen: running along the way network like the engine does near waypoints, which can come later with proper NPC navigation.

## Consequences

- Cowards and beaten NPCs run away from the hero instead of freezing.
- In tight places a fleeing NPC can still get stuck in a corner, until NPC navigation exists.
