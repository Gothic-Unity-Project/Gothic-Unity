# ADR: Parrying and blocking like in Gothic

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-31

## Context

NPCs already played their parry and jump-back moves from the game's fight AI, but every hit still landed, and the hero had no way to block at all. In the original game a parry stops a melee hit from the front, a jump back dodges it, and animals bite through a parry.

## Decisions

1. **A parry stops a melee hit from the front, within 45 degrees,** like the engine. It holds against humans, and against monsters only when they fight with a weapon (orcs, skeletons); animals bite through. The weapons clash with the game's sound.
2. **A jump back dodges any hit from the front.**
3. **The VR hero blocks with both hands on one melee weapon and both triggers held,** the way Gothic's parry animation holds the weapon at grip and blade. Not chosen: a block from the weapon's position alone - it would trigger by accident while walking with a raised sword.

## Consequences

- Fights with guards and orcs take longer, as in the original; wolves and other animals still have to be dodged.
- Spells and arrows go through a block, as in the original.
