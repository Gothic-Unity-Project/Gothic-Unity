# ADR: NPC faces move like in Gothic

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-31

## Context

NPC faces were frozen: the mouth didn't move while an NPC talked, the scripts' moods did nothing, and blinking was an unfinished option that was off, because each face animation replaced the whole face (a blink wiped out a talking mouth). In the original game the face animations of a head play on top of each other: a mood, the talking mouth and blinking at the same time, each fading in and out. BoroBongo's experimental branch has this working; this ports it.

## Decisions

1. **Face animations blend on top of each other like the engine,** each with its own fade in and out from the game files, one per kind (a new mood replaces the old one, the mouth and the blink have their own).
2. **The mouth moves while an NPC speaks a line,** and stops with the line.
3. **The scripts' face moods and repeated face animations work,** as do face animations started by body animations (pain, eating).
4. **Every NPC blinks on its own,** like in the original. The unfinished blinking option is gone.
5. **Faces only move within 12 meters of the player, and only while something changes.** Each moving face rewrites its whole head mesh every frame, which is costly on a Quest 2; nobody sees a face from afar. Not chosen: moving every face in the world, like the original does on a monitor.

## Consequences

- Talking NPCs look alive, faces blink and show the scripts' moods up close.
- A talking or blinking face near the player costs a head mesh update per frame; a held mood costs nothing after its fade in.
- Still open: lip sync to the actual words (the original doesn't have it either, the mouth loops while talking), and morph animations of items (a bow's string).
