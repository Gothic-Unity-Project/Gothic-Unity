# ADR: Torch in the hand

- Status: proposed (PR #511)
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Technical details: Docs/world-lighting.md

## Context

In VR a torch could only be carried, never lit, so mines and caves stayed dark. In Gothic, lighting a torch swaps it for the burning torch item. That item's look is a small world file holding the torch, its fire and a light: Gothic 1's light reaches 15 m, Gothic 2's 10 m, and both flicker through a list of warm colors.

## Decisions

1. **The trigger of the hand holding a torch lights it or puts it out.** It keeps burning when let go. Not chosen: lighting it at a fire, because Gothic doesn't need a fire either.
2. **Fire, light range, color and flicker come from the burning torch's own file, not from values we picked.** G1, G2 and mods get their own torch without extra work, like in OpenGothic. Not chosen: values tuned by eye or a light preset, because those drift from the game. If a mod lacks the burning torch, the torch can't be lit, which matches the original engine.
3. **There is one light that follows the hero (the hero light), shared by the torch and the Light spell. The one lit last gets it, and the others go dark.** Each extra moving light costs shader work on every pixel, which is expensive on Quest 2. Only the current holder can move or switch off the hero light, so putting out an older torch no longer switches off the newer one. Not chosen: several moving light slots, because of that per-frame cost.

## Consequences

- A second lit torch, or the Light spell cast while a torch burns, takes the light. The older torch keeps its fire but gives no light until it is lit again.
- The flicker can only change as often as the "Light refresh" setting refreshes the light, so it is slower on Low.
- Torches are not saved: they are unlit after a load.
- NPCs carrying torches at night don't light up through this.
